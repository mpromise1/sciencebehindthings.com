The 3rd Kinematics Equation Derivation

Eliminating time parameter \(t\) from foundational constant-acceleration frameworks using definitive symbolic substitutions.

📐 Technical Appendix: Symbolic Flow

To isolate final velocity purely as a function of position displacement, we begin by referencing the standard definition of acceleration and velocity under constant structural gradients.

Step 1: Isolate Time from Velocity Definition
$$v_f = v_i + a \cdot t \implies t = \frac{v_f - v_i}{a}$$
Step 2: Substitute into Position Function
$$\Delta x = v_i \cdot \left(\frac{v_f - v_i}{a}\right) + \frac{1}{2}a \cdot \left(\frac{v_f - v_i}{a}\right)^2$$
Step 3: Expand and Isolate Final Velocity Squared
$$2a\Delta x = 2v_i v_f - 2v_i^2 + v_f^2 - 2v_i v_f + v_i^2 \implies v_f^2 = v_i^2 + 2a\Delta x$$
