The Philosophy Behind the Physics

Most physics courses are built on a dangerous lie: that physics is a game of matching variables to memorized formulas.

When students rely on basic equation sheets, their conceptual understanding breaks down the moment a problem introduces multi-stage motion, variable forces, or changing coordinate systems. Generic AI tools and textbook solution manuals compound the problem by providing answers without explaining the core integration choices required to get there.

Science Behind Things was founded to eliminate formula guesswork. We treat mechanics as a strict system of logical derivations built directly from first principles using calculus.

Our Technical Standard

We do not use messy imperial units or shortcuts meant for introductory, algebra-based tracks. Every curriculum block, derivation pipeline, and premium practice solution manual on this platform operates strictly within metric SI units. This ensures total compatibility with standard elite educational benchmarks, college-level prerequisites, and advanced engineering physics frameworks.
